Thales Costa Silva

I'm a postdoctoral research associate in the Automatic Coordination of Teams (ACT) Lab in the Department of Computer Science at Brown University, where I work with Nora Ayanian.

Before Brown, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the GRASP Lab at the University of Pennsylvania, working with M. Ani Hsieh. I received my M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais in Brazil, advised by Luciano Pimenta and Fernando Souza.

Research

I work on multi-robot systems: distributed coordination and control, coverage and planning under communication constraints, and learning-based and robust control. My background is in time-delay systems and stability analysis of nonlinear systems. Some papers are highlighted.
